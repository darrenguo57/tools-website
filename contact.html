Frequently Asked Questions

Is UseEasyTool free to use?

Yes! All our tools are completely free to use. No registration or payment required.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All processing happens in your browser. We never upload or store your data on our servers.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes! We welcome suggestions. Send us an email or open an issue on GitHub.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all messages within 48 hours during business days (Monday through Friday, 9 AM - 6 PM Beijing Time). Feature requests and bug reports may take longer if they require investigation or consultation with our development team.

Before You Reach Out

To help us assist you faster, please check our FAQ page first. Many common questions about tool functionality, data privacy, browser compatibility, and usage limits are already answered there. If your question is about a specific tool, please also read the How to Use section on that tool's page.

Bug Reports

When reporting a bug, please include: the tool name, your browser and version, the steps to reproduce the issue, the expected result, and the actual result. Screenshots are greatly appreciated. The more detail you provide, the faster we can fix the issue.

Feature Requests

We love hearing what tools you wish existed. When suggesting a feature or new tool, describe the problem you are trying to solve and how a specific tool would help. We prioritize new tools based on user demand and technical feasibility.

Q: Can I request a custom tool?

Yes! Visit our Custom Tool page to describe what you need. We build custom tools for users who want something specific that our standard collection does not cover.

Q: Do you offer an API?

Currently, all our tools run in the browser and do not have API endpoints. We are exploring API options for popular tools. If this interests you, let us know via the contact form.

Q: Can I embed your tools on my website?

Not currently. However, you are welcome to link to any of our tool pages. If embedding is important to you, contact us and we will prioritize it.
