How to Use

About This Tool

The Crop & Rotate tool provides precise control over image framing and orientation. Cropping removes unwanted outer areas to focus on subjects, change aspect ratios, or meet platform-specific dimension requirements. Rotation fixes tilted photos and adjusts orientation for both screen display and print layouts. The tool operates entirely in the browser, ensuring your images never leave your device.

Input Guide

Upload an image by dragging it to the canvas area or clicking the upload button. For cropping, click and drag on the image to define a selection rectangle, then click "Apply Crop". For rotation, use the preset buttons (-90, -45, +45, +90) or enter a custom angle. The flip buttons mirror the image horizontally or vertically. The reset button restores the original image state.

Output

The edited image is rendered on a canvas and can be downloaded as a PNG file. The output preserves the dimensions of your crop selection or rotated canvas. Image quality is maintained using high-quality canvas rendering. The tool displays the new dimensions and file size estimate before download. All edits are non-destructive until you choose to save.

Tips & Notes

  • Plan your crop ratio ahead — common aspect ratios include 1:1 (social media), 16:9 (widescreen), and 4:5 (Instagram portrait).
  • Rotating by arbitrary angles (not 90-degree multiples) creates a larger canvas to fit the tilted image.
  • Cropping before rotating yields different results than rotating before cropping — plan your sequence.
  • Always keep the original file; browser-based editing does not preserve the full quality of professional RAW files.

Common Use Cases

Photographers straighten horizon lines in landscape shots. Social media creators crop images to platform-specific aspect ratios. E-commerce managers frame product photos consistently across a catalog. Real estate agents correct vertical perspective in building photographs. Graphic designers extract specific regions from stock photos for composite layouts. Document scanners fix skewed page orientations in captured images.
