How to Use

About This Tool

The Loan Calculator helps you understand the true cost of borrowing by computing monthly payments, total interest paid, amortization schedules, and annual percentage rate (APR) breakdowns. It supports fixed-rate loans including mortgages, auto loans, personal loans, and student loans. The amortization table shows how each payment is split between principal and interest over the loan term.

Input Guide

Enter the principal amount (the initial loan balance), annual interest rate as a percentage, and loan term in years or months. Optionally specify additional monthly payments to see how they accelerate payoff. For mortgages, you can include property tax, homeowner's insurance, and PMI to calculate the total monthly housing payment (PITI).

Output

The primary output is the fixed monthly payment amount. The tool also displays total interest paid over the life of the loan, the total cost of the loan, and the payoff date. The amortization schedule breaks down every payment by principal, interest, and remaining balance. You can export the schedule as a CSV file for spreadsheet analysis.

Tips & Notes

  • A small increase in monthly payments can significantly reduce total interest — experiment with extra payment scenarios.
  • The APR includes fees and points, while the stated interest rate does not — compare loans using APR.
  • Shorter loan terms have higher monthly payments but substantially lower total interest costs.
  • This calculator assumes fixed rates; adjustable-rate mortgages (ARMs) require recalculation after each rate change.

Common Use Cases

Home buyers compare 15-year versus 30-year mortgage options and evaluate the impact of larger down payments. Car shoppers determine affordable monthly payments within their budgets. Graduates assess student loan repayment strategies under different income-driven plans. Financial advisors demonstrate debt consolidation savings to clients. Real estate investors calculate cash flow for rental property financing.
