Frequently Asked Questions

What if my tool idea is simple?

Simple tools are welcome! Even a $5 contribution helps cover our time. We'll add useful tools to the platform for everyone.

How long does development take?

Typically 1-2 weeks for simple tools, longer for complex ones. We'll email you with updates if you provide your email.

What if I can't pay?

You can still submit your idea! Select the smallest amount or $0. We prioritize paid requests but consider all suggestions.

Will the tool be public?

Yes, custom tools are added to UseEasyTool for everyone to use. If you need a private tool, mention it in your request.
