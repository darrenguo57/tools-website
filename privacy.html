Privacy Policy

Overview

At UseEasyTool, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools.

Data Processing

All our tools process data locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit your files, text, or any data you input into our tools to any server. Your data never leaves your device.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your preferences (such as language and theme settings). These are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our website. This information is anonymous and does not identify individual users.

Advertising

We may display advertisements on our website. Third-party ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect our website. However, since all tool processing happens in your browser, the security of your data is primarily under your control.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
