3. How We Use Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide a better browsing experience, and to supply information to the owners of the site. We use both first-party cookies (set by us directly) and third-party cookies (set by our trusted partners and service providers).

First-Party Cookies: We set the following types of first-party cookies on your device:

Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the basic functioning of the Site. They enable core features such as page navigation, secure access to protected areas, and language and theme preference settings. Without these cookies, the Site cannot function properly.

Preference Cookies: These cookies allow us to remember your choices and preferences, such as your preferred language (English or Chinese), your selected theme (light or dark mode), and other customizable settings. They enhance your experience by providing a more personalized interaction with the Site.

Analytics Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously. They allow us to identify which pages are most popular, how users navigate through the Site, and where improvements may be needed. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify any individual user.

Third-Party Cookies: In addition to our own cookies, third-party services that we use on the Site may also place cookies on your device. These include advertising cookies used by ad networks to deliver personalized advertisements, and social media cookies that enable sharing features. These third-party cookies are governed by the respective privacy policies of the third-party providers, not by this policy.

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and set preferences for certain websites. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site and limit your ability to use certain features.

Cookie Consent Banner: When you first visit our Site, a cookie consent banner will appear at the bottom of the page. You have three options:

  • Accept All: Allows all cookie types (necessary, analytics, and advertising).
  • Necessary Only: Allows only essential cookies required for the Site to function.
  • Manage: Opens a detailed preferences panel where you can individually enable or disable analytics and advertising cookies.

Your cookie preferences are stored in your browser's local storage and will be remembered for future visits. You can update your preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for useeasytool.com or by using the "Manage" option if the banner is still visible.
